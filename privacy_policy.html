Privacy Policy for CloneCat

Last updated: August 27, 2026

CloneCat ("the App") is an Android app that manages apps across device users (work profile, private space, clone, secondary users) and creates home-screen shortcuts, using the on-device Shizuku framework.

Data Collection

CloneCat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party. The App does not have a backend server.

Data Access

The App accesses the following data, all of which stays on your device:

  • List of user profiles on your device (work profile, private space, clones, secondary users)
  • List of installed apps and their icons, for the purpose of displaying and managing them
  • Shortcut configuration you create, stored locally in app preferences

Shizuku Permission

CloneCat uses Shizuku to perform privileged operations (listing users, installing/managing apps across users, launching apps). This access is granted explicitly by you through the Shizuku app and can be revoked at any time. CloneCat does not use root access.

Data Sharing

CloneCat does not share any data with third parties. No analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs are included in the App.

Data Storage

All data used by the App (shortcut preferences, cached app info) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences and cache mechanisms. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Children's Privacy

CloneCat does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
